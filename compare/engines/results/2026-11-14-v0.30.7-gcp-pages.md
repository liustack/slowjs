- Machine: GCP t2d-standard-4, AMD EPYC 7B13, four cores without SMT, Linux 7.0.0-1017-gcp, linux/amd64. Every process pinned to CPU 0 with `taskset -c 0`, the whole machine held for it, GOMAXPROCS=1, TZ=UTC, 4 GiB cap.
- liustack/quickjs v0.30.7 and this comparison at `v0.30.7-8-g449ffb859` (the engines were built from that tree), Go go1.27.4, binaries built with `CGO_ENABLED=0 -pgo=off -trimpath`.
